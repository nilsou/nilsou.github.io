How can I help?

Hineni is a Hebrew prayer app for iPhone and iPad, made by Nils Hayat, an ex-Apple engineer who loves praying and cares deeply about design.

Questions, feedback, or a problem?
Email hineni@nilsou.com

Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and what happened. For a passage or calendar issue, include the prayer, tradition, selected date, calendar region, and location city. Please leave out personal details you don't need to share.

What language are the prayers in?

The main prayer text is Hebrew. The interface and directions support English, Hebrew, French, Spanish, Italian, Russian, Portuguese, and German. A small set of life-occasion prayers includes translations; the app is not a fully translated or transliterated siddur.

How do trial and lifetime access work?

You explicitly start a free 14-day trial. After it ends, continued reading requires a separate one-time lifetime purchase at the price shown in the app. Nothing renews or charges automatically. There is no subscription. Reinstalling does not restart the trial.

How do I restore access?

Use the same Apple Account that acquired the trial or lifetime purchase. In Hineni's Settings or access screen, choose “Restore purchases.” Connect to the internet when restoring. If access is still missing, email the address above with the message shown in the app. Do not send Apple Account credentials or payment details.

How do I read offline?

Prayer texts and calendar data are bundled with the app. Existing verified lifetime purchases and active trials support offline access. Starting purchases, restoring them, searching for cities, and optional location/geocoding services may need a connection.

How do I change the reading experience?

Open Reading Appearance from a prayer page to choose from six text sizes and paper, white, or night mode. Pagination is manually optimized for each size. Save a passage using “Save this place,” then find it in the Saved tab. Your tradition, masculine or feminine wording for morning prayers, calendar region, and location can be changed in Settings.

What if a time or calendar cue looks wrong?

Check the selected date, city, and Israel/diaspora calendar setting. Refresh the location when you travel. Community customs vary, and calculated times do not replace your community's guidance near a prayer deadline. Please report a mismatch with the details requested above.

Does it cover every holiday?

Hineni includes daily and Shabbat prayer, with Rosh Chodesh and the three pilgrimage festivals, plus blessings for food and travel and prayers for marriage, birth, mikveh, and childbirth. Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur require a separate machzor and are not included. The app supports Ashkenaz, Hasidic Sefard, and Sephardic; community-specific practices can differ.

How is my information handled?

No app account, advertising, or analytics. Preferences and saved passages stay on your device. Optional location and purchases use Apple services. Read the privacy policy for details, including retention, backups, and deletion.
