Siddur Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Siddur: A Time For Prayer (also called Hineni in the app) is developed by Nils Hayat. You can read the bundled prayers without an app account. The developer does not receive your prayer preferences, saved places, reading history, or location. Siddur has no advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, or developer-operated server.

Preferences and saved places

Siddur stores your prayer tradition, personal wording, calendar region, reading theme, text size, screen-awake and automatic-opening preferences on your device. It also stores bookmarked passages and the last prayer, passage, tradition, and reading date so you can return to your place. These choices and reading records can reveal religious preferences. They are not sent to the developer.

The app bundles its prayer texts and calendar. Reading them does not download content or send a record of the passages you read.

Optional location and Apple geocoding

You can select a preset city without giving Siddur access to your device's location. Siddur requests location permission only when you choose “Use my current location” in Settings. It requests a single location while the app is in use, with kilometer-level desired accuracy. The coordinates returned by iOS may be more precise; Siddur does not round them before saving them.

Siddur sends that location to Apple's geocoding service to find a city name and time zone. It saves the latest latitude, longitude, city name, and time zone on your device to calculate prayer times offline. It does not continuously track you or request background location. Apple processes location and geocoding requests under its own privacy policy.

Purchases and free trial

Apple's App Store and StoreKit handle the free trial, lifetime purchase, and restoration. Siddur requests product information and reads verified transaction information, including product identifiers, purchase dates, and revocation status, to determine reading access. Apple's locally available transaction information also supports offline access.

The developer does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account credentials through the app. Apple processes purchases and retains transaction records under its own policies. Deleting Siddur does not erase Apple's purchase records or restart a trial; restoring purchases can recover the original trial or lifetime entitlement.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Preferences, saved places, the last reading position, and the latest saved device location remain on your device until you change or remove them, or delete the app. There is no automatic expiration. You can remove individual saved passages in the Library's Saved section or by tapping the saved-place control in the reader.

You can turn off location permission in iOS Settings at any time and select a preset city in Siddur. Turning off permission or selecting a preset city does not erase coordinates already saved in the app. A new location request replaces the saved device location. To remove all of Siddur's local preferences, reading records, and saved coordinates, use Delete App in iOS Settings; offloading the app keeps its data.

iOS may include app data in device or iCloud backups according to your Apple settings. Restoring a backup may restore that data. Manage or delete backups separately in Apple's settings. The developer has no copy of your local app data to retrieve or delete. Apple's service and transaction records have separate retention rules controlled by Apple.

External links and Apple services

Opening a website from the app, including source, license, and privacy links, connects through your browser to that website. The website receives ordinary web-request information such as your IP address and browser details and applies its own privacy policy. This is separate from reading bundled prayers offline.

Apple's privacy policy describes its processing for location, geocoding, the App Store, backups, and other Apple services: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.
